# INSEET login logo

Original asset downloaded from https://www.inseet.in/inseet-logo.png and saved locally at `frontend/public/brand/inseet-logo.png`.

The logo artwork and colors are preserved. A light backing keeps the navy and blue logo readable against the full-screen office photograph. Authentication buttons, links, focus states and headline accent use corresponding blue/navy colors. The original logo has a pale photographic background, so it is displayed as provided rather than treated as a transparent asset.
